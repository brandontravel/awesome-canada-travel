# Awesome Canada Travel [![Awesome Travel](https://srv-cdn.himpfen.io/badges/awesome-travel/awesometravel-flat.svg)](https://github.com/brandonhimpfen/awesome-travel)

> A curated list of **resources, apps, communities, guides, and tools** for traveling in Canada — especially helpful for **solo travelers**, **digital nomads**, and **independent explorers**.

## 🇨🇦 General Travel Information

- [Destination Canada](https://www.destinationcanada.com/) – The official tourism marketing agency for Canada.
- [Travel.gc.ca](https://travel.gc.ca/) – Canadian government travel information and advisories.
- [Canada.ca Immigration](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/immigration-citizenship.html) – Official site for visas, eTAs, and border services.

## 🧳 Solo Traveler Resources

- [SoloTraveler.org](https://www.solotraveler.org) – Tips, safety guides, and destination ideas for solo travelers in Canada and worldwide.
- [Brandon’s Curated Shortlist of Trips for Solo Travelers](https://trips.solotraveler.org/) – Monthly updated list of no or low single supplement tours.
- [Essential Language Phrases for Solo Travelers](https://www.solotraveler.org/essential-travel-phrases/) – Common travel phrases for communication in English and French.

## 🌎 Digital Nomads in Canada

- [Digital Nomads Network](https://www.digitalnomadsnetwork.org) – A community of remote workers and nomads with Canada-specific content.
- [Work in Canada (IRCC)](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/work-canada.html) – Info on digital work visas and remote work legality.
- [Nomad List – Canada](https://nomadlist.com/canada) – Rankings, cost of living, and reviews for digital nomads in Canadian cities.

## 🏘️ Accommodation & Co-Living

- [Airbnb Canada](https://www.airbnb.ca/)
- [TrustedHousesitters](https://www.trustedhousesitters.com/) – Great for long-term stays across Canadian cities.
- [Couchsurfing](https://www.couchsurfing.com/) – Meet locals and stay for free.
- [HomeExchange Canada](https://www.homeexchange.com/) – Swap homes with Canadians across provinces.

## 🚆 Transportation

- [Via Rail](https://www.viarail.ca/) – National passenger train service across Canada.
- [Rideshare Canada](https://www.ridesharing.com/) – Long-distance ride-share listings across provinces.
- [Rome2Rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/) – Compare travel options between any two Canadian destinations.

## 🏞️ Notable Places to Visit

- **Banff & Jasper National Parks** – [Parks Canada](https://parks.canada.ca/)
- **Quebec City** – A charming, French-speaking walled city.
- **Tofino, BC** – Surf town on Vancouver Island, ideal for slow travel.
- **Cape Breton, NS** – Scenic drives, Gaelic culture, and hiking.

## 🛠️ Tools & Apps

- [Transit App](https://transitapp.com/) – Real-time public transit schedules.
- [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/canada) – Hiking and biking trail maps.
- [XE Currency](https://www.xe.com/) – Currency conversion for travelers.
- [WeatherCAN](https://weather.gc.ca/) – Official Environment Canada weather forecasts.
